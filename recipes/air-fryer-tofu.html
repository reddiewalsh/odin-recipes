Air Fryer Tofu

Description

This recipe is for a easy and healthy dish of Tofu, made with some simple ingredients and air-fried.

It’s recommened that you freeze and thaw the tofu before making this recipe to improve the texture. It will also increase the absoption of your marinade.

Ingredients

  • Extra-firm Tofu
  • 2Tbsp Soy Sauce
  • Olive Oil
  • 1 Tbsp Sesame Oil
  • Garlic

Steps

  1. Press the Tofu for atleast 15 minutes: It's important to remove excess moisture.
  2. Slice the Tofu: Keep the pieces evenly-sized for better results.
  3. Marinate the Tofu: dizzle with mixture of soy sauce (2Tbsp), sesame oil (1 Tbsp), Olive Oil (1 Tbsp), and garlic (1 clove, minced).
  4. Air Fry the Tofu: for 10-15 Minutes at 190°C (375°F).
