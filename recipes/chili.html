Simple Chili

Description

This is a simple, but hardy meal that is great for the cold winter months.

Ingredients

  • Beef (1lb)
  • Onion (1)
  • tomato sauce (1 can)
  • stewed tomatos (1 can)
  • kidney beans (1 can)
  • Chili Powder
  • Garlic Powder
  • Salt
  • Black Pepper

Directions

  1. Cook the Beef and Onions in a large saucepan until meat is brown and onion is tender.
  2. Stir in Tomato Sauce, Kidney Beans, and Stewed Tomatoes
  3. Stir in Chili and Garlic Powder.
  4. Bring to a boil and then let simmer on low for 15 minutes
